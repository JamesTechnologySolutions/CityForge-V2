Welcome to CityForge

Shape a living 3D city. Lay roads first, zone land beside them, then provide electricity and services to grow your population.

  • Left-click a tile to build or bulldoze.
  • Right-drag to orbit the camera. Middle-drag to pan.
  • Mouse wheel zooms. WASD moves the view. Q / E rotates.
  • Residential, commercial and industrial zones need nearby roads and power.
  • Water, parks, schools, clinics and public safety improve residential happiness.
  • Raise or lower taxes to balance city growth and the budget.
  • Your city autosaves every in-game month. Save and load manually at any time.

CityForge is an independently developed city-building game by James Technology Solutions.
